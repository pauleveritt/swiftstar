# SU.6f: after 7b

A small cycle (`docs/sdd.md`): one doc, no spec. It takes the punch
list's "after 7b" bullets, now that 7b is on main and merged into `su-ui`
(`ffb2f89`).

## Decisions

The owner made D2 and D4 (SU.9's D3, SU.6e's D4) and filed D1; D1's
method and D3 follow from the facts below.

**D1. Toolbar menus get the toolbar's own insets.** Drop
`.menuStyle(.borderlessButton)` and the label's 8 pt padding, so the
folder, model and context menus draw as standard toolbar pop-ups, as in
Xcode.

**D2. View › Bigger (⌘+), Smaller (⌘−) and Actual Size (⌘0)** step the
font size through its five slots; Actual Size returns to 18.

**D3. The rename:** `transcriptFontSize` becomes `fontSize` everywhere
(the environment value, `DefaultsKey`'s case, the properties), since it
sizes the whole window. The stored key is already `fontSize`.

**D4. Transcript rows fade under the composer.** The composer and status
bar become a `safeAreaBar` at the transcript's bottom edge, with a soft
scroll edge effect, in place of the `VStack` and its dividers.

**Dropped:** the `ToolbarSpacer` between the session and Inspector
buttons. 7b added it (`SessionToolbar.swift:34`).

## Facts this rests on

- **Probes on 2026-10-13** (`SessionToolbar`'s "Running" preview; each
  reverted):
  1. label padding 8 → 24 with `.borderlessButton`: no change at all
  2. no menu style: native insets on both sides, chevron included
  3. no menu style and no label padding: even insets, the closest to
     Xcode's
  So SU.4's "toolbar menus ignore `.font`" note was about this style:
  `.borderlessButton` drops the label's padding as well.
- **`safeAreaBar(edge:)`** insets the view and extends its scroll edge
  effect; `scrollEdgeEffectStyle(.soft, for: .bottom)` gives the blur
  (Apple docs, checked for SU.6e).
- **The rename touches 18 files, 55 uses,** all mechanical.
- **`SwiftStarApp`'s commands** are a `.commands` block with
  `ToolbarCommands` and a Session menu (7b's layout).
