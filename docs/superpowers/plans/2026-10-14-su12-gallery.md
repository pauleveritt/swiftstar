# SU.12: the gallery app

A small cycle (`docs/sdd.md`): one doc, no spec. The owner, 2026-10-14:
find a way to work that is more Xcode-like than launching a build that
needs the GPU and locks the engine.

## Decisions

The owner agreed the direction on 2026-10-14 and granted Xcode Screen
Recording; D3 follows.

**D1. A second app, `SwiftStarGallery`,** with its own scheme: the real
window (`MainView`, its toolbar and the menus) over the preview fixtures'
running controller. It never starts the engine; its settings are its own
defaults domain (the process name).

**D2. One window and one set of menus for both apps.** The commands move
from `SwiftStarApp` into `SwiftStarUI` as `SwiftStarCommands`, so the
gallery can't drift from the app. The app delegate (Dock menu, quit
waiting on the engine) stays in the app.

**D3. The agent's loop:** `RunProject` on the gallery scheme, then
`Tools/window-shot.sh SwiftStarGallery out.png`, then read the image.
ui-loop says so: a toolbar or menu change is checked this way, not in the
canvas.

## Facts this rests on

- **Fixture controllers never spawn:** `EngineController(model:)` sets
  `isFixture`, and `start()`, the catalog and the lists return early
  (`EngineController.swift:200-262`).
- **The fixtures are public-ready:** `previewRunning`, `[Conversation].preview`
  in `PreviewSupport/PreviewFixtures.swift`, internal and not `#if DEBUG`.
- **Capture works:** `screencapture -x -o -l <window id>` on an Xcode window,
  2026-10-14, after the grant (`CGWindowListCopyWindowInfo` gives the id).
- **The canvas lied about the toolbar** (SU.6f's Result), which is why D1
  needs the real window.

## Result

Done 2026-10-14 on `su-ui`, in two commits: the plan (`77a2ac7`) and this
one, which holds both tasks.

**Seen working:** `RunProject` on `SwiftStarGallery` launched in about 7 s
with no engine; `Tools/window-shot.sh` captured its window. The capture
shows what the canvas didn't: the model and context menus' text against
the capsule's left edge, as the owner reported.

**Suites:** `just test` and `just integration`, with no other test run
going. No tests are new: the commands moved unchanged, and the gallery is
the existing fixture.

**Diverged:**
- **`EngineController.gallery()` is just `previewRunning`,** made public
  under a name for the gallery; the fixtures stay internal.
- **The Dock menu and quit handling stay in `SwiftStarApp`'s delegate:**
  the gallery has no engine to wait for.

**The owner's first ⌘R, 2026-10-14:** "nothing launched". The gallery was
running, but as a background app with its window behind Xcode: a bare
executable needs `setActivationPolicy(.regular)` and `activate()`, which
the app's delegate does and the gallery's lacked. It has its own delegate
now. Its console's CoreUI "unable to find a bundle" faults and `linkd`
connection errors are noise from running without a bundle; the app logs
them too.
